# Assembly card

One page. Pieces are named as in the print files; the number in brackets is the file the piece comes from.

## Before you start

- Pins: cut 1.75 mm filament into 12 mm lengths. Every pin hole is 2 mm wide and 6 mm deep, so half a pin goes each side.
- Glue: thin CA for pins, gel CA for faces. 5 minute epoxy for the tongue Ga0 and the big plinth faces.
- Dry fit every step with pins and no glue first. Glue only once it closes flat.
- Every piece goes on in one straight push (the direction is in brackets). If it will not go, you are on the wrong step.

## Splints: which goes where

A splint is a hidden strip glued inside the hollow, half into each piece. Each one is shaped to the wall it sits on, so it
only fits one place. Dots on one end give its number, and the dotted end goes into the piece you glue it into first.

## Statue

1. Gbb0bb1a0 (07) and Gbb0bb1a1 (06) onto Gbb0bb1b (10). Push sideways (x), pins.
2. Gbb0bb0 (12) into the bottom of Gbb0bab (13), pins. Then that pair down onto Gbb0bb1b (z), pins.
3. Glue splints 1 and 2 in. Lower Gbb0baa (11) straight down onto them. It slides down along the face of Gbb0bab: glue that face as it goes.
4. Glue splints A and B in. Slide Gbb0a0 (08) and then Gbb0a1 (03) on from the front (y), one after the other.
5. Gbab0a (10) onto Gbab0b (09) (y), pins. Gbb1 (09) onto Gbab0a (x), pins.
6. That group and Gbab1 (12) up into the right wing Gbaa (12) (z), pins.
7. Glue splint 3 in. Push the whole right wing group onto the body from the side (x), pins.
8. Tongue Ga0 (06) and Ga1 (02) onto Gbb0a1 (y), pins. Tongue with epoxy, keep paint off the slide.
9. Head: Haa0, Haa1 and Hb (02) onto Hab (01) (x), pins. Glue splint 4 in. Lower the whole head onto the body (z), pins.

Glue every face that touches, pins or not. Faces with no pins are fine: each of those pieces is held by pins on another face.

## Plinth

1. Each top onto its own walls (z), pins: P0aa_top (24) on P0aa (19), P0ab_top (23) on P0ab (18), P0ba_top (22) on P0ba (17), P0bbb_top (21) on P0bbb (16).
2. Front: P0aa onto P0ab (x), pins.
3. Back: P0bba (20) onto P0bbb (x), pins. Then the deck P0bba_deck (20) onto P0bbb_top, slid in from the P0ba side (x).
4. That group onto P0ba (x), pins.
5. Back half onto front half (y), pins.
6. Door frame P1 (14) down into the door hole, glue only. Then the door (15).

## Statue onto plinth

Set the statue on the landing and fit the 4 M3 screws from below. No glue, so it comes off again.
